CoinDrop FAQ

What is CoinDrop?

CoinDrop is a free Android app by CoinX (gspteck) where you earn crypto daily: press the button to claim rewards, complete simple tasks, contribute to development and refer friends. A Premium membership adds bigger button rewards, secondary tokens and infinite energy.

Is CoinDrop the same as coindrop.in?

No. CoinDrop (coindrop.website) is not coindrop.in. CoinDrop is the airdrop app on Google Play published by gspteck; coindrop.in is a separate, unrelated site.
