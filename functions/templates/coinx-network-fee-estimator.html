Crypto network fee estimator

Work out what a transfer will cost in network fees before you send it. Enter the numbers your wallet shows: for Ethereum, the gas the transaction uses and the base fee and tip in gwei; for Bitcoin, the transaction size in virtual bytes and the fee rate in sat/vB. Add a coin price if you want the fee in your own currency. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent or saved, and CoinX does not set network fees.

Ethereum (gas)

21,000 is the gas a plain ETH transfer uses (ethereum.org). Token transfers and contract calls use more; copy the figure your wallet shows.

Enter the gas used and the base fee to see the fee.
Bitcoin (sat/vB)

Size depends on how many inputs and outputs the transaction has, not on the amount sent.

Enter the size and fee rate to see the fee.

How it is calculated. Ethereum: fee = gas used × (base fee + priority fee), and 1 gwei = 0.000000001 ETH. Bitcoin: fee = size in vbytes × fee rate in sat/vB, and 1 sat = 0.00000001 BTC. On Ethereum your wallet may reserve a higher maximum fee; the unused part is refunded. A failed Ethereum transaction still pays for the gas it used.
