How it works

From the coin you have to the coin you want

Four steps in one simple flow, from choosing coins to sending the deposit.

  1. Pick the pair

    Choose the coin you have and the coin you want. Each pair shows its minimum and maximum amount.

  2. Check the amount

    Enter how much you want to swap and see the estimated amount you receive. Choose Fixed Rate or Float Rate.

  3. Add your address

    Paste the receiving address for the coin you want, on the network shown (for example TRC20 or BEP20).

  4. Send and confirm

    Tap “Swap now”, send the exact amount to the deposit address (QR code and copy buttons included), then tap “Sent”.

Features

Everything you need for a quick swap

Rebalance holdings, move into a coin you follow, or keep your favorite pairs close. Swappr is a place to swap — not a charting desk.

Swap a wide range of cryptocurrencies, from stablecoins like USDT to Bitcoin, Dash, Cosmos and more, without hopping between apps.

Trust & safety

Swap carefully, every time

The app literally says it in the address field: “Always double check!”

Always double-check

Before you confirm, check the pair, the amount, the destination address and its network, and any rate or fee shown in the app. Crypto transfers to a wrong address or network usually can’t be reversed.

No promises of profit

Crypto markets move. Swappr does not guarantee profits, yields, APYs or returns, and rates, speed and network conditions can change.

Swaps by an exchange partner

Swaps are processed by FixedFloat, shown as “powered by FixedFloat” on the deposit screen.

Data safety

On Google Play’s Data safety section, Swappr declares that no data is collected and that device or other IDs may be shared with third parties. Declarations can change, so check the live listing.

FAQ

Questions about Swappr

What is Swappr?

Swappr is a free Android app by GSPTeck for swapping cryptocurrencies in one place. You pick the coin you have and the coin you want, check the estimated amount and limits, add your receiving address and confirm, without learning a full exchange terminal.

Is Swappr free?

Yes. Swappr is free to download on Google Play. The app contains ads. Any rate or fee for a swap is shown in the app before you confirm.

Which coins can I swap?

Swappr supports a wide range of cryptocurrencies, for example USDT (TRC20), Bitcoin, Bitcoin Cash, Cosmos, Dash, PancakeSwap (BEP20) and Avalanche (C-Chain). The list in the app is the current one, and every pair shows its minimum and maximum amount.

What is the difference between Fixed Rate and Float Rate?

Swappr lets you choose a fixed or a float rate for a swap. A fixed rate locks the amount you are quoted for that order; a float rate follows the market until the swap is executed, so the final amount can be higher or lower than the estimate. Check the amount shown in the app before you confirm.

Does Swappr guarantee profits or returns?

No. Crypto markets move. Swappr does not guarantee profits, yields, APYs or returns, and rates, speed and network conditions can change. Always double-check the pair, the amount, the destination address and any fee or rate shown in the app before you confirm.

Is Swappr the same as CoinDrop or IOU?

No. Swappr (com.gspteck.swappr) is GSPTeck's crypto coin-swap app. It is not CoinDrop, the Solana airdrop app, and not IOU, the personal money-you-owe tracker.

Is Swappr available on iPhone?

Swappr is currently available for Android on Google Play.
