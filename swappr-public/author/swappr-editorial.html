Swappr Editorial (GSPTeck)

The articles on swappr.website are written and maintained by the GSPTeck editorial team at GSPTeck, the studio behind Swappr, a free Android app for simple multi-coin crypto swaps (a CoinX project by GSPTeck).

The guides cover instant crypto swaps: picking the right network, pasting the right address type, memos and destination tags, fixed vs float rates, tracking a swap and what can pause one for KYC. Every article shows in its byline the date it was published and the date its facts were last verified.
