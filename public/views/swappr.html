Swappr is a free Android app for simple multi-coin swaps. Pick the pair, check the estimated amount and limits, paste your address and send — no exchange terminal to learn.

How it works

From the coin you have to the coin you want

  1. Pick the pair

    Choose the coin you have and the coin you want. Each pair shows its minimum and maximum amount.

  2. Check the amount

    Enter how much you want to swap and see the estimated amount you receive. Choose Fixed Rate or Float Rate.

  3. Add your address

    Paste the receiving address for the coin you want, on the network shown (for example TRC20 or BEP20).

  4. Send and confirm

    Tap “Swap now”, send the exact amount to the deposit address (QR code and copy buttons included), then tap “Sent”.

Crypto markets move: Swappr does not guarantee profits, yields or returns. Always double-check the pair, amount and destination address before you confirm. Swappr is not CoinDrop and not IOU.
